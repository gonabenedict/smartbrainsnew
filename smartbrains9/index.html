Our story

About Smartbrains Academy

Six decades of academic ambition, character, and community.

Our history

Founded in 1962 on a single classroom block, Smartbrains has grown into a full day school for grades 1 through 9, educating over 740 students from more than 30 countries.

Through six decades we've held to one idea: that a school's first duty is to know each student well enough to help them become who they're capable of becoming.

Director's message

"Every choice we make — from class sizes to the shape of our pastoral system — starts from the same question: what does this particular student need to thrive?" — Lennox Mtengo, Director

HOME / ACADEMICS

A rigorous, balanced curriculum

Academics

From foundational learning in Grade 1 to competency-based pathways in Grade 9, delivered through Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC).

Curriculum overview

Smartbrains Academy follows the CBC, Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum. Grades 1–6 (Lower & Upper Primary) build core literacy, numeracy, and life-skill competencies; Grades 7–9 (Junior Secondary) broaden into subject-based learning and career pathway exploration ahead of transition to Senior Secondary.

Departments

Mathematics

Foundational numeracy through applied and pathway-level mathematics.

Sciences

Integrated science, Biology, Chemistry, and Physics, taught in dedicated specialist labs.

English & Humanities

English, Kiswahili, History & Citizenship, Geography, Religious Education.

Modern & Classical Languages

French, Spanish, Mandarin and Latin from Grade 7.

Design, Technology & Computing

Pre-technical studies, computer science, and engineering.

Creative & Performing Arts

Music, drama, fine art, and photography.

Physical Education

Applied sport science alongside 18 competitive teams.

Academic calendar

  • Term 1September – December
  • Term 2January – March
  • Term 3April – July
  • AssessmentsCBC formative & summative assessments: ongoing, termly

Assessment information & learning support

Learners are assessed continuously under the CBC through formative and summative assessments that track competency development, alongside the national Grade 6 (KPSEA) and Grade 9 (KJSEA) assessments. A dedicated Learning Support department provides one-to-one and small-group support for learners with additional learning needs.

Academic resources — past assessments, revision guides, and subject reading lists — are available through the student portal.
